# Research Overview

My research operates at the intersection of computational modeling, data-driven systems, and human-computer interaction. I focus on developing scalable methods that turn complex datasets into actionable insights.

## Primary Research Themes

### 1. Scalable Computational Frameworks
Developing lightweight, performant architectures for processing and analyzing high-dimensional domain data in real time.

### 2. Human-Centered Algorithmic Interfaces
Designing visual interface abstractions that help non-specialist domain experts interact with complex machine learning outputs.

### 3. Open & Reproducible Science
Building open-source tools and data pipelines that adhere to FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) data standards.
